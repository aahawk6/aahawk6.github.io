The through line

Research moves fast. The data has to keep up.

Research teams rarely hand over a clean spec. They bring a hypothesis, a prototype device and a deadline. My job is to turn that into milestones, staffing plans and quality bars, then build the tooling that proves the data is right.

I started as an electrical engineer, which still shapes how I work: instrument the system, measure what matters, and fix root causes instead of symptoms. That applies whether the pipeline runs through a hardware lab, a collection site on another continent, or an external annotation vendor.
